Recommended actions

  1. Prioritize high-risk category and time combinationsAt the 100-transaction threshold, focus on combinations such as online shopping at 20:00–23:59 and grocery POS at 00:00–03:59.
  2. Prioritize $250+ transactions for reviewApply additional verification when high amount appears with other risk signals: observed fraud rates were 17.86% for $500+ and 5.76% for $250–$499.
  3. Review high-volume, above-average merchantsStart with merchants in the matrix’s top-right quadrant, then inspect their category, time and amount patterns before taking action.
  4. Apply state-volume safeguardsFilter all states by a minimum transaction count before comparing fraud rates; validate low-volume results before acting.
  5. Use combined risk rulesCombine amount, category, time, merchant and geography rather than relying on one warning sign. Treat the one-month findings as initial indicators only.
